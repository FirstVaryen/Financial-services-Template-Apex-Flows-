Why Investment and Loan are separate objects, not one with a Type field: an investment matures once and pays out; a loan disburses once and repays over a schedule of installments, with its own risk states (In Arrears, Defaulted) that an investment never has. Forcing both into one object means half the fields on every record are meaningless. Splitting them also means a client who only signs up for lending never has to deploy or look at Investment__c at all — see the build guide's Part 15 for how that plays out at deploy time.
